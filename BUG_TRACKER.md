## Open (not changed)

| ID | Sev | Area | Finding | Why not fixed / what is needed |

| O-01 | HIGH | Gold instrument | Setups are labelled **XAUUSD** but computed on Yahoo **`GC=F`**, the COMEX front-month gold **future**. Futures trade above spot by the basis (cost of carry, typically $10 to $60 and changing daily), the continuous series jumps at each contract roll (which can draw false structure and distort gold backtests), and Yahoo futures data is delayed. Your XAUUSD entry, stop and targets will not match these levels. The alert text warns, the levels do not adjust. | Needs a spot XAUUSD feed (your broker's API) or a live basis correction. A data-source decision, so not changed. Until then treat gold levels as approximate and re-read them on your XAUUSD chart. |
| O-02 | HIGH | Backtest integrity | The 18 Nov 2026 hold-backs (stop under 7%, zone under 2/2, 1H volume under 2x, Mixed regime; intraday after 14:00, stop under 0.5%; options and gold zone 2/2) were **chosen from the same trades** they are then evaluated on (287 swing, 999 intraday, 24 options, 48 gold intraday; `holdbacks.py` says "all in-sample, one market phase"). The "with hold-backs" results are therefore optimistic. The window starts 1 Apr 2026 (about 7.5 months) and there is no development / validation / unseen split. | Changing or removing rules is your decision. Needed: freeze the rules today and judge them only on trades after today (paper trading is exactly that), or a walk-forward test on history before 1 Apr 2026. |
| O-03 | HIGH | Options backtest | Option P&L uses **modelled** premiums (Black-Scholes on the index path, previous day's VIX held constant, no skew, no bid-ask). This is a substitute for real premiums, which your audit brief says not to rely on. The page discloses it. | No free historical option prices. Do not judge the options strategy on this tab; use paper trades with real quotes (Angel One live chain is already read in the live scan). |
| O-04 | HIGH | Journal risk limits | Daily, weekly and monthly loss limits sum P&L by the trade's **entry date**. A swing trade entered last week and stopped out today does not count toward today's or this week's limit. Shown: a Rs 3,000 loss (1.5% of capital) closed today leaves the banner at 0% and green. | The trade format (shared with Trade Insight and the Supabase row) has no exit-date field. | O-05 | MEDIUM | Journal | One exit per trade: partial exits (half at T1, a quarter at T2) can only be recorded through the manual "net override". Charges are typed in by hand (no automatic STT / stamp / exchange / GST). | Feature work in the shared format; documented. |
| O-06 | MEDIUM | Consistency | The journal counts a trade within ±0.1R as breakeven and leaves it out of win rate and profit factor; the backtest counts any R > 0 as a win and R <= 0 as a loss. The journal orders its equity curve and drawdown by entry date, the backtest by exit. So the two win rates and drawdowns are not directly comparable. | Definitions are a choice; documented so you compare like with like. |
| O-07 | MEDIUM | Costs | Delivery cost 0.30% of entry per round trip and intraday 0.08% are flat assumptions. The options model (Rs 40 + 0.15% of premium turnover) covers the 0.15% STT on the sell premium in force since 1 Apr 2026 but omits GST on brokerage (about Rs 7 a round trip). | Calibrate both from your Angel One contract notes (`COST_PCT`, `SEGMENTS` in `backtest.py`).
